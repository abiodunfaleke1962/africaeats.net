Our Story

A father's recipe, the family's passion

African Cuisine — better known as Dad's Spicy Chicken — was born from a father's love of cooking and brought to life by the whole family. Every plate carries generations of West African wisdom: recipes passed down, refined, and made fresh every single day.

For our family, food is how we say welcome. It's the spice that fills the kitchen, the pot that never empties, and the reason neighbors keep coming back. We pour that same passion into every order — the kind of food that makes you feel at home.

From our family's table to yours: come hungry, leave full, and taste what we love.

"Good food brings people together."
